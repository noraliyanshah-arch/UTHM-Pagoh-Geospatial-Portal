About the Project

About the Pagoh Hub

Universiti Tun Hussein Onn Malaysia (UTHM) Pagoh Campus forms part of the Bandar Universiti Pagoh development. This project applies GIS technologies to support spatial visualisation and analysis of the campus and surrounding residential college environments through integrated vector and raster datasets.

The mapping framework integrates satellite imagery, drone-based raster data, and ground-referenced information to support accurate spatial representation and visualisation within a Web GIS environment.

R Our Project Story

This project was developed collaboratively by a multidisciplinary student team as part of the BWS 33603 Geospatial Science course, integrating geospatial analysis, cartographic design, and web-based GIS to support spatial understanding of the UTHM Pagoh Campus and Residential Colleges.

The objective of this project was to translate complex geospatial data into an intuitive Web GIS to support spatial exploration within UTHM Pagoh Campus and Residential Colleges.

"Our goal was simple: to make complex geospatial information intuitive for everyone who sets foot in Bandar Universiti Pagoh."
